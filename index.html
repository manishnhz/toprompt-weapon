SEC-04

COLLECTOR FAQ

How do you grade book condition?
We use industry-standard grading: Fine (minimal wear), Very Good (light shelf wear), Good (reading copy). Each listing includes detailed condition notes and high-resolution photos of dust jacket, binding, and interior.
How do you authenticate signed copies?
Signatures verified by certified bibliographers with reference exemplars. Provenance chain documented. Certificate of Authenticity issued for all signed editions. Third-party authentication available on request.
What is your return policy?
14-day return window for items not matching condition description. Full refund minus shipping. High-value items ($5K+) require pre-approval. Condition must match original listing — no reader wear accepted on returns.
Do you offer appraisal services?
Formal appraisals for insurance or estate purposes: $150 per title. Bulk collection appraisals quoted on request. We also purchase collections — contact us with inventory list for offer.
How should I store rare books?
Climate-controlled environment: 65-70°F, 30-50% humidity. Store upright on shelves, never stacked flat. Use acid-free sleeves for dust jackets. Avoid direct sunlight. Archival storage boxes recommended for high-value items.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes. International shipping via FedEx or DHL with full insurance. Customs declarations handled. Buyer responsible for import duties. High-value items require signature confirmation. Typical transit: 5-10 business days.
